Hyderabadi Biryani

Ingredients

Chicken 1 kg bone-in chicken (pieces cut medium-sized)
Basmati Rice 500 g
Onions 2 large
Rice 3 to 4 cups long-grain Basmati Rice, washed and soaked for 30 minutes
Ghee and Oil 3 to 4 ts of Ghee and cooking oil for richness
FryingOnions 3 to 4 large onions, thinly sliced and deep-fried until golden brown (birista)
Herbs 1 cup total of fresh mint leaves (pudina) and fresh coriander cilantro leaves
Green Chilies 6 to 10 green chilies (slit or made into a coarse paste)
Ginger-Garlic Paste 1 ts of ginger-garlic paste
Yogurt 1 cup of plain yogurt (curd)
Spices 1 ts turmeric powder, 2 ts red chili powder, 1 ts garam masala, 1 ts coriander powder, 1 ts cumin powder
Whole Spices 4 to 5 green cardamom pods, 4 to 5 cloves, 1-inch cinnamon stick, 1 bay leaf, 1 ts black peppercorns
Lemon Juice 2 ts of fresh lemon juice
Saffron A pinch of saffron strands soaked in 2 ts of warm milk
Salt To taste
Biryani masala 2 ts of biryani masala (optional)

A Brief on Making

1. Heat ghee and oil in a large pan over medium heat.

2. Add the whole spices and sauté for a minute.

3. Add the onions and fry until golden brown.

4. Add the ginger-garlic paste and cook for a minute.

5. Add the chicken pieces and cook until they change color.

6. Add the spices and cook for 5 minutes.

7. Add yogurt and cook for 10 minutes.

8. Add the soaked rice and mix gently.

9. Cover and cook on low heat for 20 minutes.

10. Garnish with fried onions and fresh herbs.
